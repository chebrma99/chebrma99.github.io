Engagement Optimization in Large-Scale Video Platforms: A Systems Review of Metrics, Ranking, Experimentation, Latency, and Feedback Loops

This systems-oriented review examines video engagement optimization across five connected layers: engagement metrics, multi-stage ranking architectures, online experimentation, latency-sensitive serving, and personalization feedback loops.

The central argument is that engagement is an end-to-end systems property. Ranking quality, serving responsiveness, metric design, experimental interpretation, and feedback dynamics jointly shape observed outcomes, so no single offline ranking metric is sufficient to describe system quality.
